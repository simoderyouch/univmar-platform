UNIVMAR — PHASE 12: DASHBOARD & REPORTING

STATUS

Phase 12 is complete. The dashboard now turns existing commercial and
operational data into a live management view.

LIVE METRICS

The dashboard calculates, from the database:

- Live order value, order count, and average order value.
- Collected payments and outstanding customer receivables.
- Available, reserved, and damaged inventory in square metres.
- Draft, sent, accepted, and soon-expiring quotations.
- Confirmed, preparing, and partially delivered orders.
- Planned and dispatched deliveries.

OPERATIONAL FOLLOW-UP

The dashboard also highlights delivery work scheduled in the next seven days,
inventory records with 5 m² or less available, and open customer invoices
ordered by due date. Each signal links to the relevant operational workspace.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

No chart uses invented values. Every dashboard number is calculated from the
same orders, quotations, invoices, deliveries, and inventory records that the
team works with elsewhere in UNIVMAR.

DATA

The reporting module is read-only. It introduces no new business tables; its
DashboardService composes a consistent snapshot from existing modules and
exposes GET /api/v1/dashboard/overview.
