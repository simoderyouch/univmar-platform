UNIVMAR — PHASE 8: ORDERS & STOCK RESERVATION

STATUS

Phase 8 is complete. It is the point at which an accepted quotation becomes a
real customer order and physical stock is committed.

PURPOSE

The reservation workflow prevents the company from promising the same slabs or
stock quantity to more than one customer.

ORDER RECORD

An order is created once from an accepted quotation. It copies the customer,
project, commercial totals, and material lines, then records stock-reservation
allocations for each order item.

ORDER STATUSES

- PENDING
- CONFIRMED
- PREPARING
- PARTIALLY_DELIVERED
- DELIVERED
- CANCELLED

RESERVATION STATUSES

- ACTIVE
- RELEASED
- CONSUMED

ATOMIC ACCEPTANCE FLOW

1. Validate that the quotation is sent and not expired.
2. Prevent a second order from the same quotation.
3. Lock relevant inventory rows and check available stock.
4. Allocate/reserve the needed quantity across inventory records.
5. Create the order and its audit events.
6. Mark the quotation ACCEPTED.

If any check fails, the transaction fails as a whole; no incomplete order or
partial reservation is left behind. Cancelling an eligible order releases its
active reservations. Delivery consumes reservations later in Phase 9.

INTERFACE AND DATA

The Orders workspace exposes overview, items, reservations, delivery, invoice,
and history tabs. Migration V11 creates orders, order lines, reservation
records, and order events.
