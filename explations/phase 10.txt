UNIVMAR — PHASE 10: INVOICES & PAYMENTS

STATUS

Phase 10 is complete. It tracks customer receivables after a sale without
processing online payments or attempting to be a full accounting system.

PURPOSE

The financial workspace lets UNIVMAR see what each customer owes, what has been
received through normal business channels, and what remains outstanding.

INVOICE RECORD

One invoice can be created for each confirmed or fulfilled customer order. It
copies the order's subtotal, tax, transport, and grand total and stores an
optional due date and internal note.

INVOICE STATUSES

- DRAFT
- ISSUED
- PARTIALLY_PAID
- PAID
- OVERDUE
- VOID

PAYMENT RECORD

A payment records amount, received date, method, optional reference, optional
note, and creation timestamp. Supported methods are CASH, BANK_TRANSFER,
CHEQUE, CARD, and OTHER.

WORKFLOW

1. Open Invoices & payments.
2. Create a draft from a confirmed or fulfilled order.
3. Issue the invoice when it is ready to send to the customer.
4. Record each payment received outside the application.
5. The system updates the received and outstanding totals automatically.
6. When the balance reaches zero, the invoice becomes PAID.

FINANCIAL RULES

- A quotation/order cannot create more than one invoice.
- Payments can be recorded only for issued invoices.
- A payment cannot exceed the outstanding balance.
- An invoice with a recorded payment cannot be voided.
- Past-due unpaid invoices are shown as OVERDUE.

INTERFACE AND DATA

The Invoices workspace includes a receivables summary, invoice list, draft
creation, issuing, invoice detail, and manual payment ledger. The Orders
workspace Invoice tab shows the linked invoice's total, received amount, and
remaining balance. Migration V13 creates customer_invoice and invoice_payment.
