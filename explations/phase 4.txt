UNIVMAR — PHASE 4: CUSTOMERS & COMPANIES

Purpose

Phase 4 creates the commercial customer foundation used by later projects, RFQs, quotations, and orders. Future records reference one customer ID instead of duplicating names, phones, email addresses, and company data in every workflow.

Customer model

A customer is either:

- INDIVIDUAL — a private customer.
- COMPANY — a B2B company record.

Each customer stores name, optional company name, email, phone, notes, and active status. A company can have multiple named contacts and multiple addresses.

Contacts

Contacts are separate records owned by a customer. Each contact can store a name, role, email, and phone. This supports real company structures such as a purchasing manager, architect, project manager, or finance contact.

Addresses

Addresses are also owned by a customer. Each contains a label, first and optional second address line, city, country, and postal code. Labels make it possible to distinguish head-office, billing, project-delivery, and other locations later.

Backend functionality

The customer API supports:

- Creating individual or company customers.
- Creating a customer with initial contacts and addresses.
- Editing core customer/company information.
- Adding contacts to an existing customer.
- Adding addresses to an existing customer.
- Customer detail retrieval.
- Search by customer name, company name, or email.
- Filtering by customer type and active status.

Migration V5 creates the customer, customer_contact, and customer_address tables. Contacts and addresses have foreign keys to their customer, keeping ownership and future references explicit.

Frontend functionality

The Customers workspace provides:

- A customer directory with name, type, company, phone, and email.
- A create-customer form that allows an individual or company record.
- A customer detail area with tabs for Overview, Contacts, Addresses, Projects, RFQs, Quotations, and Orders.
- Existing contacts and addresses appear in their respective tabs.
- Projects, RFQs, quotations, and orders currently display “No records yet”; those workflows are implemented in later phases.

Future integration

Phase 5 projects, later RFQs, quotations, and orders will store a customer ID. This keeps customer information centralized, lets sales staff update it once, and provides a consistent commercial history on the customer detail page.

Validation

Backend package compilation passed after the Phase 4 domain, API, and V5 migration were added. The frontend TypeScript and Vite production build also passed.
