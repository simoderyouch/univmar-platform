UNIVMAR — PHASE 7: QUOTATION MANAGEMENT

STATUS

Phase 7 is complete. It converts a customer requirement into a priced
commercial proposal.

PURPOSE

A quotation establishes the commercial offer: materials, quantities, prices,
discounts, tax, transport, payment terms, expiry date, and total.

QUOTATION RECORD

Each quotation has a generated number, customer, project, optional source RFQ,
sales agent, expiry date, transport amount, payment terms, notes, revision
number, and priced line items. Item lines retain a material and variant snapshot
so the offer remains understandable even if the catalogue later changes.

STATUSES

- DRAFT
- SENT
- ACCEPTED
- REJECTED
- EXPIRED

WORKFLOW

1. Select a submitted RFQ.
2. Pre-fill the customer, project, materials, and quantities.
3. Enter unit prices, discounts, taxes, transport, expiry date, and terms.
4. Save the quotation as a draft, then send it to the customer.
5. A sent quotation can be accepted and converted to an order in Phase 8.

CALCULATIONS

The backend calculates line net values, tax values, subtotal, tax total, and
grand total. It rejects acceptance of an expired quotation.

INTERFACE AND DATA

The Quotations workspace presents both a list and a commercial-document detail
view. Migration V9 creates quotation records and lines; V10 adds the catalogue
variant reference used by the later order-reservation workflow.
