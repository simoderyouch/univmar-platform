PHASE 14 — QR / BARCODE INVENTORY

Goal
Give each received inventory position (lot/bundle) and each individual slab one stable QR label. A warehouse worker can scan it on a phone or with a handheld scanner and immediately verify the live record before moving, cutting, or dispatching it.

Included
- Opaque UUID-backed QR label for inventory positions and individual slabs.
- Real PNG QR image generation, ready for label printing.
- Mobile-friendly “Scan labels” workspace: accepts a scanner input, label code, or pasted QR URL.
- Scan result shows material, variant, lot/bundle, warehouse, location, stock quantities, slab dimensions, current status, and related order/project reservation when present.
- Label issuer returns the same label if a record already has one, avoiding duplicate labels.
- QR image endpoint can be loaded for printing without exposing commercial data; record lookup itself remains authenticated.
- Environment setting UNIVMAR_QR_SCAN_BASE_URL controls the URL encoded in generated labels.

Main API
POST /api/v1/labels                              { targetType, targetId }
GET  /api/v1/labels?targetType=...&targetId=...
GET  /api/v1/labels/scan/{code}
GET  /api/v1/labels/{code}/qr.png

Workflow
1. Open Scan labels.
2. Issue a label for a slab or a received lot/bundle and print it.
3. Attach it to the physical stock.
4. Scan before handling material to verify its live location, quantity, and reservation state.

Security note
The QR image is public only as an image. The QR token is opaque; its warehouse, customer, order, and stock data can only be resolved through the authenticated internal lookup endpoint.
