UNIVMAR — PHASE 6: RFQ (REQUEST FOR QUOTATION)

STATUS

Phase 6 is complete. It records customer material requirements before pricing
or stock commitment.

PURPOSE

An RFQ is the sales team's starting point for a possible stone sale. It records
what the customer needs, for which project, and by when, without reserving any
physical inventory.

RFQ RECORD

An RFQ has a generated number, customer, project, requested date, delivery
location, notes, a creator, and one or more material lines. Each line captures
the material/variant description, requested quantity, dimensions or processing
notes where required.

STATUSES

- DRAFT
- SUBMITTED
- UNDER_REVIEW
- QUOTED
- CANCELLED

WORKFLOW

1. Choose a customer and one of that customer's projects.
2. Add the requested material lines and quantities.
3. Save a draft or submit it for commercial review.
4. Create a quotation from a submitted RFQ.
5. The RFQ becomes QUOTED when a quotation is created.

INVENTORY RULE

The RFQ screen can show available material information for the sales team, but
an RFQ never reserves stock. Reservation happens only after a quotation is
accepted and becomes an order in Phase 8.

INTERFACE AND DATA

The RFQs workspace provides a list, status filtering, draft/submission flow,
details, requested-material lines, attachments, and an event timeline.
Migrations V7 and V8 create RFQ records, their item/attachment relationships,
and their event history.
