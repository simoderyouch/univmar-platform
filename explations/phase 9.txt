UNIVMAR — PHASE 9: ORDER PREPARATION & DELIVERY

STATUS

Phase 9 is complete. It manages the physical fulfillment of confirmed customer
orders.

PURPOSE

Reservations protect stock for an order, but they do not reduce physical
on-hand inventory. Delivery dispatch is the controlled moment at which stone
leaves the warehouse.

DELIVERY RECORD

Each delivery has a generated number, order, scheduled date, notes, status,
timestamps, and one or more order-item quantities.

STATUSES

- PLANNED
- PREPARING
- DISPATCHED
- DELIVERED
- FAILED
- CANCELLED

WORKFLOW

1. Select a confirmed or active order.
2. Plan one or more delivery quantities from its unconsumed reservations.
3. Start preparation if needed.
4. Dispatch the delivery.
5. Confirm that it arrived, or mark an unshipped delivery failed/cancelled.

STOCK RULE

On dispatch, the system consumes the corresponding active reservations,
decreases on-hand stock and reserved stock, and writes an ORDER_DELIVERY stock
movement. Partial deliveries are supported; the order becomes DELIVERED only
once every reserved quantity is consumed and confirmed.

INTERFACE AND DATA

The Deliveries workspace provides planning, details, preparation, dispatch,
delivery confirmation, failure, and cancellation controls. Migration V12 adds
delivery and delivery-item tables and tracks consumed reservation quantity.
