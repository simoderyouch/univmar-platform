PHASE 13 — INDIVIDUAL SLAB TRACKING

Goal
Track high-value marble and stone slabs as individual physical pieces, not only as aggregate m² inventory. A sales user can identify the exact slab a customer selected and reserve it for the correct order line.

Included
- Stone slab record: unique slab number, linked received inventory item, length, width, automatically calculated m², photo, cost, and inspection notes.
- Physical identity retained from stock: material, variant, thickness, lot/bundle, warehouse, and location.
- Handling lifecycle: AVAILABLE, HELD, RESERVED, SOLD, DAMAGED, CONSUMED.
- Visual slab gallery with dimensions and storage location, plus a detailed right-side record panel.
- Exact-slab reservation: only live order lines with the identical material variant appear; backend also validates the variant and that selected slab area does not exceed the order line's active inventory reservation.
- Canceling an order releases its selected slabs. Completing delivery marks its selected slabs SOLD.
- Automated integration test for creating, reserving, and releasing a slab through order cancellation.

Not included
- Cutting optimization / remnants.
- Barcode or QR scanning.
- Multi-slab allocation assistant.
- Customer-facing slab selection portal.

Main API
GET    /api/v1/slabs
POST   /api/v1/slabs
GET    /api/v1/slabs/{id}
POST   /api/v1/slabs/{id}/hold
POST   /api/v1/slabs/{id}/reserve       { orderItemId }
POST   /api/v1/slabs/{id}/release
POST   /api/v1/slabs/{id}/damage

Workflow
1. Receive stock normally in Inventory.
2. Register the physical slab and link it to that received inventory position.
3. Add a photo and inspection notes when useful.
4. A salesperson opens the exact slab, chooses a compatible open order line, and reserves it.
5. The slab follows the order: released on cancellation and sold after fulfillment is completed.
