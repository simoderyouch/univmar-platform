PHASE 16 — FABRICATION / PROCESSING

Goal
Track a custom stone order through the workshop from site measurement to ready-for-delivery handover.

Included
- Fabrication job linked to an active customer order, with priority, due date, measurement notes, technical drawing URL, and workshop notes.
- Controlled workshop stages: MEASUREMENT → DRAWING → MATERIAL_ALLOCATED → CUTTING → FINISHING → QUALITY_CONTROL → READY.
- The API prevents skipped stages and prevents READY until every planned operation is complete.
- Ordered fabrication operations: cutting, polishing, honing, edge finishing, drilling, sink cut-outs, custom dimensions, and quality checks.
- Assign material to a job from an inventory position, individual slab, or reusable remnant.
- Visual fabrication board with one column per real workshop stage.
- Job detail drawer with stage rail, operation timeline, measurements/drawing, assigned material, and progression actions.
- Automated integration test covering job creation, invalid skipped transition, material allocation, completed operations, and ready handover.

Main API
GET  /api/v1/fabrication
POST /api/v1/fabrication
GET  /api/v1/fabrication/{id}
POST /api/v1/fabrication/{id}/advance       { status }
POST /api/v1/fabrication/{id}/operations
POST /api/v1/fabrication/{id}/operations/{operationId}/complete
POST /api/v1/fabrication/{id}/materials

Workflow
1. Create a fabrication job for an active order.
2. Record the measurement and attach the technical drawing link.
3. Assign the exact slab, remnant, or inventory position to be worked.
4. Complete the planned workshop operations in order.
5. Advance the job one stage at a time, ending at READY after quality control.

Not included
- CNC machine integration.
- Cutting optimization/nesting.
- Automated material m² consumption from a fabrication operation.
- Staff schedules or time tracking.
